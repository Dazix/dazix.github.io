Vibecoding

Small apps and experiments. The web apps run in the browser, no backend; CAELIS is on Google Play.

  • Indoor Navigation

    Office navigation PWA with on-device visual localization (MobileNet), QR markers, AR compass and step tracking.

  • CodeNotchBar

    GNOME Shell extension showing AI coding agent usage limits (Claude, Copilot, Codex, Cursor) as a notch at the top of the screen. Source on GitHub.

  • GnomeAgentBuddy

    GNOME Shell extension: a blob in a notch that shows your AI coding agent sessions and lets you approve permissions from there. Source on GitHub.

  • CAELIS

    Android time-lapse that shoots through your own camera app, plus a Sun, Moon and night-sky AR planner that knows the real horizon.
